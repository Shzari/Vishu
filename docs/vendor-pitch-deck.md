# Vishu Vendor Pitch Deck

## 1. Vishu Puts Your Shop Online

Vishu.shop helps fashion vendors sell beyond the limits of walk-in traffic.

Customers can discover your shop, browse products, add items to cart, and place orders from one marketplace.

**Your products stay visible even when customers are not standing in front of your store.**

---

## 2. The Market Has Moved To The Phone

Many customers check products online before deciding what to buy or which shop to visit.

A physical shop depends on location, opening hours, and people walking past the door. Vishu creates a digital shopfront that customers can open anytime.

**Walk-in traffic is local. Online visibility can reach further.**

---

## 3. The Vendor Problem

Vendors often have strong products, but the selling process is too manual.

- New customers do not always discover the shop
- Product photos, sizes, colors, and prices are repeated in messages
- Orders can get mixed between chats, calls, and notes
- Stock is hard to keep clear
- A vendor needs a professional profile to build trust

---

## 4. The Vishu Solution

Vishu gives each approved vendor a public shop profile and product catalog inside a fashion marketplace.

Vendors can show:

- Logo and cover image
- Shop description and contact details
- Products with photos, prices, sizes, colors, and stock
- Shipping and return notes
- Customer order flow through cart and checkout

---

## 5. More Places To Be Seen

Your products can appear in multiple customer paths:

- Home page sections
- Category pages
- Search results
- All shops page
- Vendor shop profile
- Product detail pages
- Customer favorites and return visits

More visibility creates more chances to sell.

---

## 6. What Vendors Manage

Vishu gives vendors one workspace for the daily selling flow.

- Add and edit products
- Upload photos
- Set price, sizes, colors, and stock
- Publish or unpublish products
- View and manage orders
- Track sales and earnings

Commercial terms are explained clearly before the shop goes live.

---

## 7. Vendor Benefits

Vishu helps vendors move from manual selling to a more professional online workflow.

- Reach beyond only walk-in customers
- Look more trustworthy with a real shop profile
- Keep product information organized
- Let customers order through cart and checkout
- Manage orders more cleanly
- Spend more time on products and customers
