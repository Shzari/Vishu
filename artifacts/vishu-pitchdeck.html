V Vishu
Problem

Buying and selling fashion online is still fragmented.

Customers want trust, speed, and discovery. Vendors want sales without rebuilding the same tools every shop needs.

The opportunity is to make a marketplace that feels simple to shoppers and operationally useful to sellers.

Customers

Too many scattered pages, unclear product quality, hard checkout, and no central account experience.

Vendors

Small sellers need product uploads, stock controls, order handling, and payout visibility without technical setup.

Admins

Marketplaces need category governance, vendor approval, product visibility, and payments settings.

Trust

Both sides need cleaner verification, security, and predictable marketplace rules.

Vishu pitch deck02
V Vishu
Solution

A two-sided fashion platform with a ready storefront and seller operations.

The current phase already covers the core marketplace loop: browse, purchase, manage account, add products, fulfill orders, and administer vendors.

Storefront

Browse products, shops, categories, favorites, cart, checkout, account, and order history.

Vendor portal

Add products, upload photos, choose brands/colors/sizes, manage inventory, process orders, and view earnings.

Admin system

Approve vendors, manage catalog values, review requests, configure payments, and protect visibility.

For vendors

A seller workspace that removes operational friction.

Product popup

One Add Product button opens a complete popup for photos, brand, gender, category, colors, sizes, price, and stock.

Variant-friendly

A single model can include multiple colors and sizes, supporting the way fashion shops actually stock items.

Orders

Vendors can confirm, ship, deliver, and track order status from their dashboard.

Inventory

Low-stock visibility and stock controls help sellers keep listings accurate.

Earnings

Seller revenue and recent earnings are visible to the shop holder.

Privacy

Vendor screens avoid exposing other vendors and use customer first/last name instead of customer email.
